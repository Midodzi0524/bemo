## 1. About this app

<APP_NAME> is a free personal budgeting and wishlist tracker. You type in your own income, spending and savings. **The app does not connect to your bank, mobile-money or card accounts.** It contains no ads.

## 2. Who can use it

You must be **16 years or older** (or 18 or older, if the app's listing says so). If you are under 18, you confirm that a parent or guardian has agreed to your use of the app. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete the data.

## 3. Information we collect

| Information | Why | How it is provided |
|---|---|---|
| Email address and account ID (from Google Sign-In or email sign-up) | To create your account and sync your data between your devices | You provide it when signing in |
| Financial entries you type in: transactions, amounts, categories, notes, budgets, wishlist items | To run the app's features and sync across your devices | You provide it |
| App settings (theme, month start day, reminder time, lock settings) | To remember your preferences | You provide it |
| Records of your consent (the date you confirmed your age and the policy version you accepted) | To show we asked for your consent and to re-ask when this policy changes | Created when you accept |
| Assets, goals and other items you choose to add (for example the value of a vehicle) | To show your net worth and goals | You provide it |
| Crash reports and basic device information (device model, Android version, app version) | To find and fix crashes | Collected automatically by Firebase Crashlytics; contains no financial entries |

You can turn crash reports off at any time in **Settings → Privacy center**.

**We do not collect:** your location, contacts, photos, SMS, advertising ID, or microphone/camera data. We do not use analytics or advertising tracking.

**Demo mode:** If you use "Try demo", the sample data stays on your device and nothing is sent to us.

## 4. How we use your information

- To provide, sync and secure the app.
- To fix bugs and improve stability.
- To respond to your support or deletion requests.
- To comply with the law.

We do **not** sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it to profile you.

## 5. Where data is stored and who processes it

Your data is stored in **Google Firebase** (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Crashlytics) operated by Google LLC and its affiliates, which acts as our service provider. Servers may be located outside Ghana, including in the United States and Europe. Data is **encrypted in transit** (TLS) and **encrypted at rest** by Google. A copy of your data is also kept on your device so the app works offline; protect your device with a screen lock and, optionally, the app's own lock.

Google's privacy information: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## 6. Legal bases (where applicable)

We process your data to perform our service to you (providing the app), on the basis of your consent (for example, notifications and the age/consent confirmation), and to meet legal obligations. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your data or account.

## 7. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your data from our systems; remaining backups and logs are purged within **30 days**. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.

## 8. Your rights

You can, at any time:

- **Access and export** your data: *Settings → Data → Export* (CSV/JSON).
- **Correct** your data: edit it inside the app.
- **Delete** your data or account: *Settings → Account → Delete account* (immediate), or request deletion at <DELETION_PAGE_URL> or by emailing <SUPPORT_EMAIL> from your registered email. We act within 30 days.
- **Object or withdraw consent**, and **complain** to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana (https://www.dataprotection.org.gh) or, if you are in the EU/UK, your local supervisory authority.

## 9. Security

We use authentication, Firestore security rules that restrict each account to its own data, Firebase App Check, encrypted transport, and optional biometric/PIN app lock. No system is perfectly secure; keep your device and Google account protected with strong credentials and 2-Step Verification.
